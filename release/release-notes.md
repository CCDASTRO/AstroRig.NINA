AstroRig for N.I.N.A. 1.0.2 is a beta release targeting N.I.N.A. 3.3.0.1069 nightly.

- Shows the AstroRig telescope icon in the Plugins page.
- Improves toolbar, equipment selection, and tooltip contrast.
- Shows saved specs when hovering over equipment names and lists occupied filter slots directly in the rig table.

Close N.I.N.A., extract AstroRig-NINA-1.0.2.zip into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\NINA\Plugins\3.0.0\AstroRig`, then restart N.I.N.A. Your existing AstroRig inventory can be reopened from the Imaging panel.
